❓ How it works

What is ResearchGraph Agent?

It helps international students and researchers turn dense academic materials into concept maps, learning gaps, seminar questions, and presentation-ready action plans.

Workflow

  1. Upload papers, lecture slides, PDFs, Word documents, images, or paste notes directly.
  2. The system extracts readable text from the material.
  3. Click "Run Agent Analysis".
  4. The Agent builds a concept graph and identifies learning gaps, seminar questions, and study actions.
  5. Review the graph, export results, and use the report for class discussion or research preparation.

Graph Controls

  • • Drag nodes to move them
  • • Scroll to zoom
  • • Click a node for details
  • • Click an edge for relationship info
